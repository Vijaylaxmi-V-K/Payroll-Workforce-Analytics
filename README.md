*# Payroll \& Workforce Analytics*



*## 📊 Project Overview*



*Payroll \& Workforce Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes employee workforce data, attendance, payroll costs, overtime expenses, and payment status.*



*The project combines Excel, MySQL, SQL, Python, and Power BI to transform raw workforce data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.*



*The objective is to help management understand workforce size, payroll expenditure, attendance performance, overtime costs, and payroll payment status across departments.*

*## 🎯 Business Problem*



*Organizations need clear visibility into workforce and payroll data to monitor employee costs, attendance, overtime, and payroll processing.*



*This project answers key business questions such as:*



*- How many employees are in the organization?*

*- What is the total net payroll expenditure?*

*- Which departments have the highest payroll costs?*

*- How consistent is employee attendance across departments?*

*- Which departments have the highest overtime costs?*

*- What percentage of payroll records have been paid or are pending?*

*- How does monthly payroll expenditure change over time?*



*---*



*## 🗂️ Dataset*



*The project uses four main datasets:*



*- \*\*Departments\*\* – department information*

*- \*\*Employees\*\* – employee and department details*

*- \*\*Attendance\*\* – employee attendance records*

*- \*\*Payroll\*\* – monthly salary, overtime, deductions, and payment information*



*### Dataset Size*



*| Dataset | Records |*

*|---|---:|*

*| Departments | 8 |*

*| Employees | 1,000 |*

*| Attendance | 12,000 |*

*| Payroll | 12,000 |*



*The payroll analysis covers monthly records from \*\*January 2025 to August 2025\*\*.*
